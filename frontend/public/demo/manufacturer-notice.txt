Northstar Home Appliances safety notice RL-2026-04

Stop using Northstar K200 electric kettles from production lot LOT-24A. A connector fitted to this lot may overheat after repeated boiling cycles. Owners should unplug the kettle and contact Northstar for return or replacement instructions. K200 units from other production lots are not included in this demonstration notice.
